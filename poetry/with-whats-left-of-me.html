With What's Left of Me

Dark and deep I went afar,

Nothing off the usual and at par,

With invisible aim as small as a bee,

I ran with what's left of me.

Breathless lungs, but a thirst for dream,

And with no mouth yet I must scream,

Imagining what it means to be free,

I crawl deeper with what's left of me.

The more I sought, the less I found,

Illusion says you're running straight, but you're running around.

Contemplating how grows a tree,

And I sat down with what's left of me.

"Alive, am I?" I thought that day,

I bowed down and began to pray.

I surrendered, fell to Thee,

"Take whatever's left of me."

Written by Piyush Yadav
