Any Safe Place to Fall?

I've been strong for far too long,

A quiet ache, a voiceless song.

I've held the world, through rise and fall,

But who will hold me when I crawl?

Is there a heart that won't withdraw,

A love that sees me, scars and all?

I'm not asking for shelter from the storm,

Just somewhere soft enough to fall.

Written by Piyush Yadav
